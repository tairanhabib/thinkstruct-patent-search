# Multilingual evaluation

Generated by `python scripts/evaluate_multilingual.py`.

- 100 patents exist in English (original), German and Chinese (machine translated copies made by `scripts/make_parallel_set.py` with opus-mt, greedy decoding).
- Query: the first claim of each patent, in English, as stored (not the title, not the abstract).
- Candidates: abstracts and description paragraphs only. Claims are excluded, so a patent cannot be found through the query text itself.
- Embedding model: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small`. Recall at k is the share of queries whose patent is in the top k. MRR is mean reciprocal rank.
- The interval is a 95% paired bootstrap interval on the difference in MRR against the first row of the same method.
- "Shared space": one query vector for all languages. "Query translated, raw scores": each passage is scored against the query translated into its language, and raw scores are merged. "Calibrated": the same, with each language's scores put on one scale first (the default in `search()`).

**Two things to keep in mind when reading the German and Chinese rows.** The copies were produced by the same opus-mt models that then translate the query, so query and document share wording more than they would with real foreign patents. And finding the right patent is only half the job: sections 3 and 4 measure whether foreign patents push out English ones, which is where the raw scores fail.

## What query translation costs per query

Translating one claim-length query (median 667 characters) into 2 languages (de, zh) with greedy decoding:

| | Median | Slowest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not cached, models already loaded (10 queries, one at a time) | 1224 ms | 3056 ms |
| Cache hit: the translation step inside `search()` in the tables above | 0.05 ms | 1.20 ms |

All queries were translated in bulk before the tables above were computed: 264,465 characters in 45 s (5,814 characters per second).

## Why the shared space fails: language bias

Cosine similarity between abstracts. If the space were truly shared, a patent and its own translation would be closer than two different patents.

| Pair | Mean similarity | 99th percentile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English abstract and a different English patent's abstract | 0.831 | 0.900 |
| English abstract and the same patent in German | 0.851 | 0.894 |
| Share of different English abstracts that score above the patent's own German copy | 27.5% | |
| English abstract and the same patent in Chinese | 0.808 | 0.854 |
| Share of different English abstracts that score above the patent's own Chinese copy | 76.5% | |

## Why raw scores cannot be merged: each language has its own score level

Mean similarity between a query and a fixed random sample of passages in the same language, almost all of them unrelated to the query. This is the level a passage has to rise above. Calibration subtracts it and divides by the spread.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short queries | 0.793 | 0.799 | 0.847 |
| Claim-length queries | 0.809 | 0.825 | 0.869 |
